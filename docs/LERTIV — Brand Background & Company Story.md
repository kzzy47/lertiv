**Headquarters:** Hillsborough County, Florida  
**Founded:** 2011  
**Primary Market:** Tampa Bay and surrounding Central Florida markets  
**Industry:** Premium Outdoor Construction & Property Enhancement  
**Business Model:** Residential + Commercial Contracting  
**Approximate Team Size:** 55–65 employees

**Primary Source of Business:** Referrals, repeat customers, builder relationships, and word of mouth

## Company Overview

LERTIV is a locally founded outdoor construction and property enhancement company based in Hillsborough County, Florida.

The company designs and builds high-end exterior environments for homeowners, builders, developers, restaurants, hospitality properties, and commercial clients throughout Tampa Bay.

Its work includes:

- Paver patios and driveways
- Custom pergolas
- Outdoor kitchens
- Pool-deck renovations
- Hardscape construction
- Landscape lighting
- Artificial turf
- Drainage solutions
- Retaining and decorative walls
- Fire features
- Commercial courtyard installations
- Outdoor entertainment spaces
- Full exterior transformations

LERTIV has become one of those Tampa Bay companies that is **far more successful in the real world than it appears online**.

Its trucks are everywhere.

Builders know the name.

Property managers know the name.

Past customers constantly recommend them.

Their crews are booked weeks in advance during peak seasons.

Yet someone discovering LERTIV through Instagram or Google for the first time would have almost no idea how significant the company has actually become.

That disconnect is now one of the company's biggest challenges.

# The LERTIV Story

LERTIV was founded in 2011 by **Adrian Veltri**, a Hillsborough County tradesman who had spent most of his twenties working around masonry, landscape construction, concrete, and exterior renovations.

Even the name has a connection to the founder.

**LERTIV is an intentional rearrangement of the letters in VELTRI.**

Adrian didn't want to build another local contracting company named after himself.

He wanted something that could eventually become larger than one person.

Something that could go on a truck.

Something employees could take ownership of.

Something that could eventually become a recognizable regional brand.

So instead of calling the company *Veltri Construction*, he rearranged his last name and created:

**LERTIV.**

At the time, there was no sophisticated brand strategy behind it.

It simply sounded strong.

And it stuck.

---

# Founder Story

Adrian grew up in a blue-collar household in eastern Hillsborough County.

His father worked in commercial maintenance and mechanical trades, while several relatives worked in construction throughout Tampa Bay.

Adrian started working summers at sixteen.

His earliest jobs weren't glamorous.

He cleaned job sites.

Loaded pavers.

Moved pallets.

Pressure-washed finished projects.

Dug drainage trenches.

Picked up materials.

Eventually, he learned installation.

Then estimating.

Then project management.

What Adrian became particularly good at wasn't simply construction.

It was understanding what customers were actually trying to accomplish.

A homeowner might ask for a patio.

Adrian saw where the family would eventually put the grill.

Where people would gather during a Bucs game.

Where lighting would be needed at night.

Where water would collect during a Florida storm.

Where landscaping would eventually interfere with drainage.

Instead of thinking about individual installations, he started thinking about **complete outdoor environments**.

That philosophy eventually became the foundation of LERTIV.

---

# The First Job

LERTIV's first meaningful project came from a neighbor in Riverview.

The homeowner wanted an old concrete patio replaced with pavers.

Adrian completed the project with two friends over several weekends using borrowed equipment and a pickup truck.

The homeowner loved it.

A neighbor walked over and asked for an estimate.

Then another.

Within several months, Adrian had enough weekend projects that he left his full-time job.

For the first few years, LERTIV grew almost entirely one driveway, patio, retaining wall, and backyard at a time.

There was no advertising department.

No sales funnel.

No CRM.

No content strategy.

No marketing agency.

Customers simply told other customers:

**"Call LERTIV."**

---

# The Reputation That Built the Company

The company gradually developed a reputation around three things:

### They show up.

In an industry where communication can be inconsistent, LERTIV became known for answering phones, giving realistic timelines, and keeping customers informed.

### They solve problems instead of selling individual services.

LERTIV crews frequently identify drainage, grading, lighting, irrigation, or structural issues that customers hadn't originally considered.

### Their finished work looks expensive.

Attention to finishing details helped move the business from smaller installations into increasingly sophisticated residential and commercial projects.

That reputation became its marketing engine.

By the late 2010s, LERTIV was regularly working on projects throughout:

**Brandon, Riverview, Tampa, Valrico, Lithia, FishHawk, Apollo Beach, Wesley Chapel, Lutz, Plant City, South Tampa, and surrounding communities.**

Eventually, referrals began carrying the company beyond Hillsborough County into Pinellas, Pasco, Polk, and Manatee County.

# LERTIV Today

What started with one pickup truck has grown into a legitimate regional operation.

Today, LERTIV operates multiple installation crews, project managers, estimators, administrative staff, and a growing commercial division.

The company handles everything from **$15,000 residential upgrades to six-figure outdoor transformations and commercial projects.**

A significant portion of its work comes from:

- Previous customers
- Customer referrals
- General contractors
- Custom homebuilders
- Realtors
- Developers
- Property managers
- Architects
- Landscape designers
- Commercial relationships

Some families have hired LERTIV three or four separate times.

A driveway becomes a patio.

The patio becomes an outdoor kitchen.

Then comes the pergola.

Then lighting.

Then the customer moves and calls LERTIV again.

This customer loyalty has allowed the business to grow without ever developing a serious marketing operation.

And that has now become a problem.

# The Marketing Problem

LERTIV has effectively built a **$10M+ company with the marketing infrastructure of a $1M company.**

Its reputation has grown faster than its brand.

The company has an recognizable logo and a solid name locally, but almost everything surrounding the identity feels disconnected.

The website was built years ago and updated inconsistently.

Photography varies wildly in quality.

Some completed projects were professionally photographed.

Others exist only as photos buried inside project managers' phones.

Social media is inconsistent.

Instagram may go untouched for three weeks and then receive six posts in four days.

Facebook primarily contains project photos with captions such as:

*"Another one completed in Riverview!"*

There is little storytelling.

Little education.

No recognizable content format.

No founder presence.

No employee storytelling.

No customer journey.

No meaningful paid media strategy.

No sophisticated email follow-up.

No retargeting.

No centralized content library.

No consistent video production.

No system for turning hundreds of completed projects into marketing assets.

# The Digital Disconnect

The problem becomes obvious when comparing LERTIV's **actual company** with its **online company**.

### In reality:

LERTIV has dozens of employees.

A fleet of branded vehicles.

Large projects.

Established builder relationships.

Years of experience.

Thousands of completed installations.

Customers who enthusiastically recommend them.

Crews working across Tampa Bay every day.

### Online:

They can look like another small contractor.

That creates an increasingly dangerous gap.

New competitors have entered the Tampa market with significantly better websites, drone footage, professional social media, paid advertising, influencer partnerships, and polished branding.

Some of those competitors are significantly smaller than LERTIV.

But online, they appear larger.

Adrian describes the situation internally as:

**"We've built the company. We just never built the media company around it."**

# Why Word of Mouth Isn't Enough Anymore

Word of mouth remains LERTIV's strongest source of business.

Nobody at the company wants to change that.

They want to **amplify it.**

The challenge is that customer behavior has changed.

Someone receives a recommendation for LERTIV.

Then they Google the company.

Then they visit Instagram.

Then they look at reviews.

Then they compare LERTIV against three competitors.

The referral may have started the customer journey.

But the digital presence now determines whether that referral becomes an inquiry.

LERTIV increasingly realizes that its online presence must finally match the reputation it has spent more than a decade building offline.
